AmniGuard Firewall is a free and open-source no-root firewall for Android.

Control internet access for individual applications and addresses over Wi-Fi
and mobile connections. The app uses Android's local VPN service to apply
rules on the device without routing traffic through a remote server.

Features:

* Allow or block internet access per application.
* Configure separate rules for Wi-Fi and mobile connections.
* Support IPv4 and IPv6 TCP and UDP traffic.
* Optional scheduled rules, lockdown mode, and boot-time startup.
* Optional traffic logging and per-address rules.
* Optional DNS and host-based blocking.
* Light and dark themes.

AmniGuard Firewall does not include advertising or analytics and does not
transmit network traffic to external services.
